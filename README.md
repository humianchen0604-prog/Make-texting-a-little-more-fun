# Make texting a little more fun

An iOS 26 Messages prototype with a working on-screen keyboard. You're texting Cherry, and three messages get their own animated bubbles:

- **"I'm so tired…"** – a grainy blue bubble whose eyelid droops shut while pencil z's drift off.
- **"I just woke up"** – a soft pink bubble that yawns, with a breath balloon swelling out of its mouth.
- **"touch some grass"** – a sunny sky bubble with a painterly meadow inside; a ladybug peeks out, then creeps into the grass and hides.

Only these three can be sent; the suggestion bar offers all three. Tap a sent bubble to replay its effect.

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step.
